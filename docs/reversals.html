What improves when total gate count reverses?

This fixed cohort contains the 30 IBM New / Qiskit comparisons that reverse total gate savings at hardware Level 1 on Manila, Lima, and Jakarta. Change the compilation level to follow those same comparisons through Levels 0–3.

Positive changes favor logical optimization. Every value is a median of five paired seed changes. Logical counts use the IBM New input and optimized circuit. Compiled counts include routing and the selected hardware optimization level. Two-qubit depth counts layers after filtering to two-qubit gates. Duration and fidelity default to relative percentage changes against each seed’s baseline, summarized by their median. Uncheck the relative-change toggle to show duration savings in microseconds and fidelity gains in percentage points.

The checkbox follows the same 15 circuit/backend pairs selected at Level 1, even when they improve at another level. Red highlighting is recalculated at the selected level using the original six metrics: total gates, two-qubit gates, total depth, SWAPs, duration, and the fidelity proxy are all equal or worse at the selected level. The CX, RZ, SX, X, ID, and two-qubit depth columns do not control which rows appear or turn red. Click a column heading to sort.

Seed-level changes
